## Methods — matched-control design

To isolate the contribution of attribution-guided localization from
structural editability, MolToken regions were compared with non-attribution
control regions selected from the same starting molecules under frozen
structural and edit-opportunity calipers. Of 178 primary attribution-selected
regions across 90 starting molecules, 92 regions (51.69%) had an eligible
matched control and were retained consistently across all five matching
seeds. These matched regions covered 65 of the 90 matching-universe parents
(72.22%). The resulting outcome analysis therefore used 65 matched parents,
with parent rather than region, seed, or candidate treated as the inferential
unit.

## Results — primary matched-control analysis

Under a fixed final evaluation budget of B=5 candidate slots, attribution-
guided regions achieved a target-window hit rate of 75.38%, compared with
65.23% for structurally and edit-opportunity matched non-attribution controls.
The parent-level paired difference was +10.15 percentage points
(95% percentile-bootstrap CI, +3.14 to +17.42 percentage points).

Strict-success rates were 64.00% for attribution-guided regions and 57.91%
for matched controls, corresponding to a parent-level paired difference of
+6.09 percentage points (95% percentile-bootstrap CI, +0.55 to +12.12
percentage points).

## Results — sensitivity analysis

The preregistered observed-only target-distance endpoint was not estimable
over the complete 65-parent cohort because 48 parent-seed-method cells
contained no filled selected candidate slots. No numerical imputation was
applied. Following the estimability clarification, a complete-observation sensitivity
analysis was defined using only the frozen observation-availability criterion.
This analysis retained 58 parents with treated and control observations
available across all five matching seeds.

Within this sensitivity cohort, mean target-distance improvement was 1.3237
for attribution-guided regions and 1.2078 for matched controls, with a paired
difference of +0.1159 (95% CI, +0.0460 to +0.1991). Mean final edit score was
0.7896 versus 0.7324, respectively, yielding a paired difference of +0.0572
(95% CI, +0.0215 to +0.0959).

## Limitations / interpretation

The matched-control analysis should not be interpreted as covering every
attribution-selected region or every starting molecule: strict matching was
available for 92 of 178 primary regions and 65 of 90 parents. The analysis
therefore supports an association between attribution-guided localization
and improved optimization outcomes within the strictly matchable cohort,
rather than a causal claim for all candidate regions. Candidate-level rates
are descriptive only; statistical inference was performed at the parent
level. The study is computational and does not constitute experimental
validation of synthesized compounds or measured logD changes.
